Design

Made to be held.

A screwless, hand-shaped case, printed in three parts. The same hand-held unit is used as the Doctor Station and as the Bed Unit.

What is on the front

Screen, switch, light, keypad, infrared window. Every opening has a job.

Exploded view

Shown separately

PCB Rev 1.7

103.3 × 70.1 mm · single-sided

The board sits inside the body, held in printed guides. It is not part of the 3D model above, so it is drawn here on its own.

Side and back

Opens without tools

The OptiLink Lift enclosure: a screwless seam and a clip-in battery cover.

Enclosure parts and dimensions

MaterialPLA+

The unit size is the overall figure printed on the enclosure drawing. It is quoted as printed, not added up from the three parts.

The front joins the body on a 2 mm seam lip with 1 mm foam tape, so the unit opens without tools.

The battery cover clips in with printed parts alone, with no magnets or screws. It uses a v5 spring latch. A v6 lever lock was designed as a sturdier option.

The case is wipeable and screwless. Use a clear sleeve for each patient, and wipe with 70% alcohol between patients.

Keys that follow the user

Function keys for the doctor, digits for the patient.

Bed Unit: digits only since firmware 4.6. The Bed Unit now uses 0 to 9, * and # alone, because the A to D column of the membrane keypad proved unreliable. Fewer keys also makes it easier for a patient.

Why a keypad

A keypad is cheaper and more durable than a touch screen. It works with gloves, gives physical feedback, is easy to clean and draws little power.

Materials and colours

Three PLA+ parts, as built.

  • Front warm ivory
  • Body blue-grey
  • Battery cover off-white
  • Material PLA+ for all three parts

Ready to hand over

A matte-black presentation kit.

The box lid is 315 x 298 x 25 mm and matte black. The sticker wraps the top and all four walls, with corner tabs.

In the box: two OptiLink units, a battery charger and a quick guide, with DOCTOR and PATIENT role stickers for the two units.

The lid carries the headline “Two-way infrared bedside communicator.”
